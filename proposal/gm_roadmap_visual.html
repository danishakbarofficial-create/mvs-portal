⚡ RFQ vs. RFP Decision

Tailored routing ensures fast quotes for catalog items and airtight compliance for large tenders.

📦 Standard RFQ (Commercial Inquiry)

  • Catalog hardware, tools, standard VIAVI / Jetting units or accessories.
  • Pre-Sales team verifies supplier stock & pricing directly.
  • Commercial quotation issued rapidly within 24–48 hours.

📑 Technical RFP (Turnkey Solutions / Tenders)

  • Complex Low Current, Telecom, passive network or multi-site projects.
  • Pre-Sales submits Technical Compliance Matrix, Datasheets & BoQ design.
  • Technical approval must be obtained prior to commercial quote submission.
MVS • Executive Controls • Revenue Assurance

Strategic Value-Adds & Management Controls

Solving Core Sales Pain Points: Stagnant Deals, Accountability Friction, Loss Analytics & Cash Aging

Slide 3 of 4
🚨 1. Stagnant Deal & Inactivity Alerts

The Problem: Sales reps client ko quotation bhej kar doosre kaamon me lag jate hain. Client follow-up na hone ki wajah se deals thandi ho kar drop ho jati hain.

Automated Visual Aging Triggers:
  • 🟡 Yellow Alert: Quote submitted > 7 days with zero client follow-up logged.
  • 🔴 Red Alert: Deal inactive > 14 days flashes on GM Executive Dashboard.
🤝 2. Commercial vs. Technical Ownership Handover

The Problem: Tenders me Sales aur Engineering ke darmiyan blame game hoti hai ke datasheet kisne banani thi aur deadline kiski wajah se miss huwi.

Dual-Track Accountability:
  • Commercial Lead: Customer relationship, pricing strategy, client meetings.
  • Pre-Sales Technical Lead: Compliance matrix, vendor datasheets, engineering approval.
📊 3. Win / Loss Reason Intelligence

The Problem: Leadership ko saal ke aakhir tak yeh clear nahi hota ke hum kis wajah se bids lose kar rahe hain.

Mandatory Outcome Categorization:
  • Competitor Pricing Lower • Technical Non-Compliance
  • Client Project Shelved / Budget Cancelled • Late Submission
💰 4. Cash Realization & Debtor Aging (DSO)

The Problem: PO aane par team celebrate karti hai, lekin invoice payment 90 din tak latak jati hai aur working capital drain hota hai.

Real-Time Cashflow Tracking:
  • Invoiced vs Collected gap analysis in real-time.
  • Aged Receivables monitoring (0-30d, 31-60d, 60+ days overdue).
