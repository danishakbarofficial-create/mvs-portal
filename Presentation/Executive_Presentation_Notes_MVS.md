## 📌 Slide / Tab 1: Sales Dashboard & Pipeline (Pre-Award Phase)
*Tab to show:* `Sales Dashboard` & `Pipeline`

### Key Talking Points:
1. **Pre-Award Focus (Sales & Tenders):**
   - Strictly tracks active inquiries, client requests, and submitted quotations before a contract/PO is awarded.
   - Clean pricing: Redundant formula columns removed; single clear **`Total Price (SAR)`** column.
2. **Executive KPIs for CEO (Engr. Mounir):**
   - **Total Active Pipeline Value (SAR)**: Total volume of bids currently out in the market.
   - **Won Projects Value (SAR)**: Success volume that moves into execution.
   - **Win Rate %**: Dynamic win-loss ratio based on decided proposals.
   - **Under Pricing & Review**: Workload currently on the estimation team.
3. **Sales Rep Performance & Expected Closing:**
   - Dedicated breakdown by Sales Representative (**Nasir, Ahmed, Wael, Asayil**).
   - **Expected Closing Dropdown**: Target closing month selection with automatic calculation of months remaining to close.

> **What to Say to Mounir:**  
> *"Engr. Mounir, from this dashboard you can instantly see our total tender exposure in the market, each salesperson's contribution, and our expected revenue closings month-by-month without digging into row-by-row spreadsheets."*

## 📌 Slide / Tab 2: Projects Dashboard & Milestone Delivery (Post-Award Operations)
*Tab to show:* `Projects Dashboard` & `Projects`

### Key Talking Points:
1. **Operational Transition (Post-Award):**
   - Once a bid is marked **"Won"**, it moves into the `Projects` sheet with its official **PO Number, PO Date, and Assigned Project Manager**.
2. **Row-per-Milestone Invoicing Architecture:**
   - Real-world projects are billed in stages, not in a single lump-sum.
   - Each project row tracks its specific billing milestone:
     - *Milestone #* (`Milestone 1`, `Milestone 2`, `Milestone 3`, etc.)
     - *Milestone Scope Description* (e.g. 20% Advance, 40% Material Delivery, 40% Commissioning)
     - *Dedicated Invoice Details*: Invoice No., Invoice Date, Invoiced Amount, and Invoice Status.
3. **Cash Flow & Receivables Control (CEO Cash Focus):**
   - Top KPI cards track:
     - **Total Awarded PO Value**
     - **Total Invoiced to Date**
     - **Total Cash Collected in Bank**
     - **Pending Receivables (Uncollected Cash)**
   - Visual bar chart comparing **Collected Cash vs Pending Client Receivables**.

> **What to Say to Mounir & PMs:**  
> *"Har project ab milestone-wise invoice aur collect hoga. Kisi project ke 3 ya 4 milestones hon, har milestone ka alag invoice number, date aur payment status track hoga taake collections kabhi delay na hon."*

## 📌 Slide / Tab 3: Project P&L & Cost Control (Talha / Accounting Integration)
*Tab to show:* `Project P&L` and bottom section of `Projects Dashboard`

### Key Talking Points:
1. **Direct Integration with Accounting (Talha's Standard Model):**
   - Adopts Talha’s 15-month standardized cost matrix (May 2025 to July 2026).
   - Separates **Operational Billing** from **Direct Cost Accounting**.
2. **17 Comprehensive Cost of Revenue Line Items:**
   - *Manpower & GLs*: Monthly Salaries, Health Insurance, Government Fees.
   - *Direct Material*: Estimated & invoiced procurement.
   - *Site Logistics & Rentals*: Staff Accommodation (EJAR), Vehicle Rentals, Building Maintenance, Tools Depreciation.
   - *Petty Cash Controls*: Detailed reconciliation categories (Consumables, Meals, Ops, Fuel/POL, Travel, Utilities via Tahir petty cash logs).
3. **Bottom-Line Profitability:**
   - Calculates **Gross Profit (SAR)** (`Revenue − Direct Costs`).
   - Calculates **Gross Margin %** (`Gross Profit / Revenue`).
   - Dynamic **Financial Performance Bar Chart** tracking Monthly Revenue vs Cost vs Profit.
4. **Executive Dashboard Link:**
   - Section 3 of `Projects Dashboard` pulls live totals from `Project P&L` to display **Cost Driver Breakdown** (Materials 50%, Salaries 22%, Logistics 13%, Rentals 9%, Accommodations 6%).

> **What to Say to Talha:**  
> *"Talha bhai, aapki P&L template ko 100% preserve karte hue humne system ka core hissa bana diya hai. Tamam 17 cost categories, GL sources aur petty cash lines mojood hain. CEO Dashboard par live margin % nazar aayega jabke aap detail monthly cost audit is sheet par kar sakte hain."*
